# Hidden Coast — Demo Script (3 minutes)

## Setup
- Open: `https://hidden-coast-xxx.a.run.app/demo` (or localhost:3000/demo)
- Split screen: left = tourist chat, right = SME dashboard (Mama Aisha)
- Have the SME dashboard open in a second tab

## The Script

### [0:00] — Hook (30 sec)
> "Every year, 2 million tourists visit Mombasa. They all go to the same
> 5 places. Meanwhile, thousands of local businesses — the best food,
> the most authentic crafts, the most unique experiences — go completely
> undiscovered. Not because they're bad. Because no one connects them."

### [0:30] — The Ask (10 sec)
> "So we built an AI concierge. Watch."

Type into chat:
> "I'm in Mombasa for 3 hours. I want authentic food, something local
> to buy, and I have KSh 3,000."

### [0:40] — Agent Works (60 sec)
Let the agent think. Show the agent trace (if you built it):
- "Parsing constraints: time=3h, budget=3000, interests=[food, crafts]"
- "Searching food in Old Town..."
- "Searching crafts near Old Town..."
- "Checking Mama Aisha: 12 portions available, 35 min prep ✓"
- "Checking Karanja: 12 sandals in stock, KSh 1500 ✓"
- "Generating itinerary..."

**Don't rush this.** Let the judges see the agents working.

### [1:40] — The Plan (30 sec)
Itinerary card appears:

YOUR 3-HOUR MOMBASA EXPERIENCE
─────────────────────────────
12:00 🍛 Mama Aisha's Kitchen
2× Chicken Biryani — KSh 900
"Family recipe, 3 generations"

13:00 🏛️ Old Town Cultural Walk
Guided by local — KSh 800

14:00 👞 Karanja Leather
Open sandals — KSh 1,500
Handmade, 12 in stock

15:00 🚕 Return to port

─────────────────────────────
TOTAL: KSh 3,200 → trimmed to KSh 2,650
(under your KSh 3,000 budget ✓)


### [2:10] — The SME Side (30 sec)
Switch to SME tab (Mama Aisha's dashboard):

> "And on the other side — Mama Aisha's phone:"

Show: `New request: 2× Chicken Biryani, pickup 13:15, KSh 900 [ACCEPT]`

Click **Accept**.

Watch stock go: `12 → 10 portions`

> "She just got a customer she'd never have found. No middleman. No commission."

### [2:40] — Confirmation (15 sec)
Back to tourist chat:
> "Mama Aisha confirmed! Your biryani will be ready at 13:15.
> Karanja is 9 minutes from Old Town — you can walk there after your
> cultural walk. Enjoy Mombasa."

### [2:55] — Close (5 sec)
> "This is what happens when a tourist's intent meets a local
> business's reality — in real time, through agents."

**[END]**
